Nimbus Sans L webfont conversion for the Eli Doggart portfolio
2026-10-09

The regular, bold, and italic fonts were converted from the actual URW Type 1
fonts used by the current LaTeX resume (NimbusSanL-Regu, NimbusSanL-Bold,
NimbusSanL-ReguItal). Original outlines and advance widths are retained;
Type 1 hinting is not carried into the converted CFF outlines. Unicode mapping
uses Adobe glyph names. Web-family naming distinguishes the conversion.
