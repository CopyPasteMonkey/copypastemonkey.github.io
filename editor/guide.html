A few useful distinctions

Details section vs. heading
A details section makes an Expand / Collapse dropdown. A heading organizes the content inside that dropdown.
Image description vs. caption
Alt text describes the picture for screen readers and when the image cannot load. A caption appears below the image. Credit / attribution appears alongside the caption.
Parent heading and moving content
Choose a parent to place a subheading under it. Moving a heading moves its paragraphs, images and subheadings together.
Saving and publishing
Save Draft keeps your work. Preview website shows your own content. Publish checks the saved draft, asks you to confirm, then updates the live website.
